## How we chose them
Each shows one clearly adult person, full length, facing the camera, against a plain studio backdrop, with
nobody else in frame and no visible logos. None of them is a celebrity or public figure, and nothing is
suggestive. Photos were picked on Unsplash itself, never from a search engine or a brand site.

## A limit we state plainly
The Unsplash License covers use of the photo. It doesn't document a model release. The hackathon rules ask
for consent from every person whose image is processed. The photographers published these for free use,
and we use them only as clearly credited sample shoppers, never to depict them doing or endorsing anything.
Shoppers' own photos are processed only after they confirm they are 18+, it is their photo, and they agree.
