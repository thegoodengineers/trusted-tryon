# Vibe spec

1. **Real-world object:** the care label sewn inside a garment. Small, woven, honest facts: what it is,
   how to treat it. Ours says what this try-on can and can't tell you.
2. **Emotion in 3 seconds:** "I can believe this."
3. **Two influences that collide:** the woven care label (narrow condensed type, stitched edge) and a
   tailor's tape measure (yellow tape with tick marks) for fit.
4. **Never mistaken for:** a glossy AI demo (no gradients, no glow, no sparkles) or a fashion
   magazine (no cream page with a serif headline).

## Tokens (all in `static/theme.css`)
- paper `#F5F7FA` page; label `#FFFFFF`; ink `#1B2340` woven navy text; muted `#4A5272`
- cobalt `#2747D9`: the one action colour (stamp ink)
- tape `#E8B923`: only the fit tape
- state: pass `#1F7A4D`, warn `#8A4F00`, fail `#B3261E`
- Type: Archivo (variable, OFL, self-hosted). Width is the personality: product title at 125 width,
  label text at 75 width. One family.
- Layout: left aligned. Gallery left, buying panel right; the care label spans under both.
  Boldness is spent once, on the label. Everything else is quiet.
